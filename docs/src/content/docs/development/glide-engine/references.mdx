**Scope.** The sources used by [Research basis and prior work](/development/glide-engine/research/).
**Verified** 2026-11-05. Links were checked on that date. Publisher DOI pages
may return 403 to automated checks; their metadata was verified through Crossref
on the same day. A source's status is part of its evidence: a vendor report is
not a peer-reviewed study, and an internal measurement is not an independent
benchmark.

## Shape and gesture typing

<a id="rubine1991"></a>

### Rubine 1991

Dean Rubine. "Specifying gestures by example." *SIGGRAPH '91*, 1991, pp. 329-337.
[DOI 10.1145/122718.122753](https://doi.org/10.1145/122718.122753).
Peer-reviewed. Feature-based, trainable single-stroke gesture recognition. The
parametric baseline against which template and shape matchers are compared.

<a id="kristensson2004"></a>

### Kristensson and Zhai 2004

Per-Ola Kristensson and Shumin Zhai. "SHARK2: A Large Vocabulary Shorthand
Writing System for Pen-based Computers." *UIST '04*, 2004, pp. 43-52.
[DOI 10.1145/1029632.1029640](https://doi.org/10.1145/1029632.1029640).
Peer-reviewed. Introduces the shape channel, the location channel, resampling
and lexicon templates for word-gesture decoding.

<a id="kristensson2005"></a>

### Kristensson and Zhai 2005

Per-Ola Kristensson and Shumin Zhai. "Relaxing Stylus Typing Precision by
Geometric Pattern Matching." *IUI '05*, 2005, pp. 151-158.
[DOI 10.1145/1040830.1040867](https://doi.org/10.1145/1040830.1040867).
Peer-reviewed. Extends geometric template matching to elastic stylus typing and
location tolerance.

<a id="zhai2012"></a>

### Zhai and Kristensson 2012

Shumin Zhai and Per-Ola Kristensson. "The Word-Gesture Keyboard: Reimagining
Keyboard Interaction." *Communications of the ACM* 55(9), 2012, pp. 91-101.
[DOI 10.1145/2330667.2330689](https://doi.org/10.1145/2330667.2330689).
Peer-reviewed. The retrospective description of the word-gesture keyboard and
the N-best candidate model.

## Probabilistic decoding and neural paths

<a id="li2010"></a>

### Li 2010

Yang Li. "Gesture Search: A Tool for Fast Mobile Data Access." *UIST '10*, 2010,
pp. 87-96. [DOI 10.1145/1866029.1866044](https://doi.org/10.1145/1866029.1866044).
Peer-reviewed. Probabilistic sequence decoding over plausible character
interpretations, ranked against indexed data.

<a id="ouyang2017"></a>

### Ouyang et al. 2017

Tom Ouyang, David Rybach, Françoise Beaufays and Michael Riley. "Mobile Keyboard
Input Decoding with Finite-State Transducers." arXiv:1704.03987, 2017.
[arXiv:1704.03987](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03987).
Preprint and engineering report. Describes the production keyboard FST decoder,
beam search, look-ahead filters and the spatial plus language score composition.

<a id="alsharif2015"></a>

### Alsharif et al. 2015

Ouais Alsharif, Tom Ouyang, Françoise Beaufays, Shumin Zhai, Thomas Breuel and
Johan Schalkwyk. "Long Short Term Memory Neural Network for Keyboard Gesture
Decoding." *ICASSP 2015*, pp. 2076-2080.
[DOI 10.1109/ICASSP.2015.7178336](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP.2015.7178336).
Peer-reviewed. LSTM emissions trained with CTC, combined with FST-based lexicon
decoding.

<a id="biju2020"></a>

### Biju et al. 2020

Emil Biju, Anirudh Sriram, Mitesh M. Khapra and Pratyush Kumar. "Joint
Transformer/RNN Architecture for Gesture Typing in Indic Languages." *COLING
2020*, pp. 999-1010.
[ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2020.coling-main.87/).
Peer-reviewed. Transformer and RNN path decoding for gesture typing, with
released synthetic trace data.

<a id="graves2006"></a>

### Graves et al. 2006

Alex Graves, Santiago Fernández, Faustino Gomez and Jürgen Schmidhuber.
"Connectionist Temporal Classification: Labelling Unsegmented Sequence Data with
Recurrent Neural Networks." *ICML 2006*, pp. 369-376.
[DOI 10.1145/1143844.1143891](https://doi.org/10.1145/1143844.1143891).
Peer-reviewed. The CTC objective and the blank-label marginalization used by the
neural path.

<a id="futo2026"></a>

### FUTO 2026

David Lee Miller and Aleksandras Kostarevas. "FUTO Swipe: Layout-Agnostic Neural
Swipe Decoding." arXiv:2606.25247, 2026.
[arXiv:2606.25247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25247).
Preprint and technical report, not peer-reviewed. Describes a layout-agnostic
encoder, geometric layout conditioning and a donated swipe corpus.

<a id="futo-model"></a>

### FUTO model card

FUTO. "FUTO Swipe model card." Hugging Face, 2026.
[huggingface.co/futo-org/futo-swipe](https://huggingface.co/futo-org/futo-swipe).
Engineering documentation. Encoder `honorable_sturgeon`, English QWERTY
decoder `magic_macaw`, optional context model, input format and the FUTO Model
Weights License.

<a id="quinn2016"></a>

### Quinn and Zhai 2016

Philip Quinn and Shumin Zhai. "Modeling Gesture-Typing Movements."
*Human-Computer Interaction* 33(3), 2018, pp. 234-280; online 2016.
[DOI 10.1080/07370024.2016.1215922](https://doi.org/10.1080/07370024.2016.1215922).
Peer-reviewed. A minimum-jerk movement model with speed-accuracy noise for
synthesizing and evaluating gesture traces.

## Language model, reranking and personalization

<a id="zhang2024"></a>

### Zhang et al. 2024

Yanxiang Zhang, Yuanbo Zhang, Haicheng Sun, Yun Wang, Gary Sivek and Shumin Zhai.
"Neural Search Space in Gboard Decoder." *EMNLP 2024 Industry Track*, pp.
1245-1254. [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-industry.93/).
Peer-reviewed. Replaces Gboard's n-gram FST language model with a neural LM in
the production decoder and reports the latency trade-off.

<a id="hard2018"></a>

### Hard et al. 2018

Andrew Hard, Kanishka Rao, Rajiv Mathews, Swaroop Ramaswamy, Françoise Beaufays,
Sean Augenstein, Hubert Eichner, Chloé Kiddon and Daniel Ramage. "Federated
Learning for Mobile Keyboard Prediction." arXiv:1811.03604, 2018.
[arXiv:1811.03604](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.03604).
Preprint. On-device federated training of a next-word language model without
exporting raw user text.

<a id="adhikary2023"></a>

### Adhikary and Vertanen 2023

Jiban Adhikary and Keith Vertanen. "Language Model Personalization for Improved
Touchscreen Typing." *Interspeech 2023*, pp. 1344-1348.
[DOI 10.21437/Interspeech.2023-276](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2023-276).
Peer-reviewed. Per-user PPM and RNN language-model adaptation, with measured
keystroke savings and word-error reductions.

<a id="sivek2022"></a>

### Sivek and Riley 2022

Gary Sivek and Michael Riley. "Spatial Model Personalization in Gboard."
*MobileHCI 2022*. [DOI 10.1145/3546737](https://doi.org/10.1145/3546737).
Peer-reviewed. Per-user key-center offsets and learned spatial covariance for tap
typing, with controls against overfitting.

<a id="guo2017"></a>

### Guo et al. 2017

Chuan Guo, Geoff Pleiss, Yu Sun and Kilian Q. Weinberger. "On Calibration of
Modern Neural Networks." *ICML 2017*. arXiv:1706.04599.
[arXiv:1706.04599](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599).
Peer-reviewed. Reliability diagrams, expected calibration error and temperature
scaling.

<a id="kimrush2016"></a>

### Kim and Rush 2016

Yoon Kim and Alexander M. Rush. "Sequence-Level Knowledge Distillation."
*EMNLP 2016*, pp. 1317-1327.
[ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/D16-1139/).
Peer-reviewed. Word-level and sequence-level distillation objectives for
sequence models.

<a id="hinton2015"></a>

### Hinton et al. 2015

Geoffrey Hinton, Oriol Vinyals and Jeff Dean. "Distilling the Knowledge in a
Neural Network." arXiv:1503.02531, 2015.
[arXiv:1503.02531](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531).
Workshop paper and preprint. Temperature-softened soft targets and model
compression. Relevant only to the internal WMLM work, not to the decoder.

## Datasets and human factors

<a id="futo-dataset"></a>

### FUTO swipe dataset

FUTO. "swipe.futo.org." Hugging Face dataset, 2025-2026.
[huggingface.co/datasets/futo-org/swipe.futo.org](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futo-org/swipe.futo.org).
Dataset, MIT licensed. Donated real swipe trajectories with session-level splits;
the repository uses its English and German subsets.

<a id="heliboard-data"></a>

### HeliBoard gesture data

The HeliBoard Gesture Data Project. Codeberg, 2026.
[codeberg.org/eclexic/heliboard-gesture-data](https://codeberg.org/eclexic/heliboard-gesture-data).
Dataset, CC BY-SA 4.0. Opt-in donated gesture strokes by batch and language, with
documented selection bias and missing context.

<a id="leiva2021"></a>

### Leiva et al. 2021

Luis A. Leiva, Sunjun Kim, Wenzhe Cui, Xiaojun Bi and Antti Oulasvirta. "How We
Swipe: A Large-scale Shape-writing Dataset and Empirical Findings." *MobileHCI
2021*. [DOI 10.1145/3447526.3472059](https://doi.org/10.1145/3447526.3472059).
Peer-reviewed and dataset. A large public shape-writing corpus with speed, error
and top-4 observations.

<a id="palin2019"></a>

### Palin et al. 2019

Kseniia Palin, Anna Maria Feit, Sunjun Kim, Per Ola Kristensson and Antti
Oulasvirta. "How do People Type on Mobile Devices? Observations from a Study with
37,000 Volunteers." *MobileHCI 2019*.
[DOI 10.1145/3338286.3340120](https://doi.org/10.1145/3338286.3340120).
Peer-reviewed. A large mobile typing study with speed and uncorrected-error
baselines; it is context for evaluation, not a swipe-decoder benchmark.

<a id="reyal2015"></a>

### Reyal et al. 2015

Shyam Reyal, Shumin Zhai and Per Ola Kristensson. "Performance and User
Experience of Touchscreen and Gesture Keyboards in a Lab Setting and in the
Wild." *CHI 2015*.
[DOI 10.1145/2702123.2702597](https://doi.org/10.1145/2702123.2702597).
Peer-reviewed. Lab and field comparison of touchscreen and gesture keyboards;
out-of-vocabulary words were a major gesture-typing failure.

<a id="soukoreff2003"></a>

### Soukoreff and MacKenzie 2003

R. William Soukoreff and I. Scott MacKenzie. "Metrics for Text Entry Research: An
Evaluation of MSD and KSPC, and a New Unified Error Metric." *CHI 2003*.
[DOI 10.1145/642611.642632](https://doi.org/10.1145/642611.642632).
Peer-reviewed. Definitions for corrected, uncorrected and total error rate, KSPC
and correction efficiency.

## Open source and patents

### AOSP LatinIME

Android Open Source Project. `packages/inputmethods/LatinIME`.
[android.googlesource.com](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/inputmethods/LatinIME/).
Source code, Apache-2.0. As checked on 2026-11-05, the open tree contains stroke
capture and trail code, not a word-level gesture decoder; the production Google
decoder is not in this tree.

### HeliBoard

HeliBoard. [github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard](https://github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard).
Source code, GPL-3.0. An AOSP-lineage IME with dictionary tooling. As checked on
2026-11-05, its documentation says glide typing needs a library that is not part
of the open repository.

### FlorisBoard

FlorisBoard. [github.com/florisboard/florisboard](https://github.com/florisboard/florisboard).
Source code, Apache-2.0. A clean IME architecture. As checked on 2026-11-05,
glide typing is planned, not shipped in the current stable release.

### OpenBoard

OpenBoard. [github.com/openboard-team/openboard](https://github.com/openboard-team/openboard).
Source code, GPL-3.0. An AOSP-lineage IME. As checked on 2026-11-05, glide typing is not supported.

### Unexpected Keyboard

Unexpected Keyboard. [github.com/Julow/Unexpected-Keyboard](https://github.com/Julow/Unexpected-Keyboard).
Source code, GPL-3.0. Lightweight IME. As checked on 2026-11-05, directional
swipes select alternate keys, but there is no word-level swipe decoder.

### FUTO Keyboard

FUTO Keyboard. [gitlab.futo.org/keyboard/latinime](https://gitlab.futo.org/keyboard/latinime).
Source-available, FUTO Source First License 1.1-kb. On-device neural swipe
decoding with a lexicon beam; the swipe models use a separate FUTO model
license.

### CleverKeys

CleverKeys. [github.com/tribixbite/CleverKeys](https://github.com/tribixbite/CleverKeys).
Source code, GPL-3.0. Claims a CTC trie-beam engine and a SHARK2-style geometric
engine. The performance claims are not independently verified here.

<a id="us7098896b2"></a>

### Swype patent

US7098896B2, "System and method for continuous stroke word-based text input."
Priority 2003. [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7098896B2/en).
Patent. Continuous-stroke word input. Patent status and claim scope require
legal review; this page is not a freedom-to-operate opinion.

<a id="us9454240b2"></a>

### Google patent

US9454240B2, "Gesture keyboard input of non-dictionary character strings."
Priority 2013. [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9454240B2/en).
Patent. Gesture selection and non-dictionary output.

<a id="us8059101b2"></a>

### Apple patent

US8059101B2, "Swipe gestures for touch screen keyboards." Priority 2007.
[Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8059101B2/en).
Patent. Directional swipe gestures for editing, not word decoding.

## Internal research artifacts

These are repository files, not publications. They are reproducible from the
scripts and notes in the repository, but they are not peer-reviewed and have not
been independently replicated.

- `wmk-glide/README.md`: folder map, datasets and the headline German result.
- `wmk-glide/state.md`: the live run log for German tuning, compounds, the
  context model and the statistical checks.
- `wmk-glide/kb-context-blend-optimizations.md`: the Glide and Typing context
  blend audit, including the OOV gap and the pool-size measurements.
- `wmk-glide/kb-student-architecture-de.md`: the student-model and distillation
  audit, including the corrected OOV piece counts and the statistical power
  calculation.
- `wmk-glide/experiments-followup.md`: the negative and fair-split follow-ups,
  including the in-sample blend caveat.
- `wmk-glide/plans/research-notes.md`: the teacher-free distillation plan and
  its external citations.

## Adding a reference

1. Choose a stable ID, lower case and no spaces.
2. Record the exact title, authors, year, venue, DOI or official URL.
3. Add the status: peer-reviewed, preprint, engineering report, dataset, source
   code, patent or internal experiment.
4. Name the claim the source supports. Do not add a source that supports only a
   general impression.
5. Check the link on the day you add it and record that date.
6. Link it from [Research basis](/development/glide-engine/research/) by its
   anchor.

A vendor benchmark is not an independent study. A preprint is not peer review.
An internal log is not a user study. Keep the label.
